Mark & Fold

Fold pages precisely without cutting

1

Place Ruler on Right Page

Position your ruler on the right page following the pattern measurements

2

Mark Top & Bottom

Make small pencil marks at the top and bottom measurements

3

Fold Both Ends

Fold both ends of the paper towards the marks you made

4
🔄

Repeat Pattern

Continue this process for each page in your pattern

5

Cut & Fold

Create dramatic 3D sculptures

1
📖

Prepare Book

Choose a hardcover book and have your cutting pattern ready. Make sure you have a stable surface to work on.

2
✏️

Mark Cut Lines

Using your ruler and pencil, carefully mark where you need to cut on each page according to your pattern.

3
✂️

Cut with Scissors

Use sharp scissors to carefully cut along the marked lines. Cut slowly and precisely for best results.

4
🔽

Fold Sections

After cutting, fold the cut sections according to your pattern to create the 3D effect. Press folds firmly.

5
🎨

Amazing Result

Continue through all pages and watch your dramatic 3D book sculpture come to life!
